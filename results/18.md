# Travel booking saga

Assumptions: the flight is ticketed with no contractual void/refund right; the hotel can be cancelled within a verified refund window. Provider capabilities and timing budgets require confirmation.

“Rollback the flight transaction” is invalid: its local transaction has committed, and a saga cannot undo the airline’s contractual commitment. Cancellation may destroy the ticket’s value without recovering money. Represent this as partial success.

## Handle the current booking

Persist `FLIGHT_CONFIRMED_HOTEL_RECOVERY`, including ticket ID, flight charge, hotel attempt ID, and failure reason. Keep the flight valid.

1. Classify the hotel outcome. A definitive rejection permits recovery; a timeout means `HOTEL_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. Query/reconcile by the original booking reference before creating another reservation. If the original booking succeeded, adopt it and complete the itinerary; reconcile any late success against current state to prevent duplicate lodging.
2. Retry transient hotel failures with bounded exponential backoff and the same idempotency key. Proposed starting policy: three attempts, with separate configurable participant deadlines; stop before supplier cancellation/booking cutoffs. Permanent rejection immediately enters recovery.
3. Seek another hotel only within previously authorized price and suitability constraints; otherwise request the traveler’s choice. Notify them that the flight is confirmed, lodging is unresolved, and the flight charge remains incurred.
4. Successful lodging yields `COMPLETED`. Exhausted recovery yields `PARTIAL_SUCCESS_REQUIRES_ACTION`, with an assigned support case. Record any merchant-funded reimbursement or supplier exception as a separate business remedy; never label it a flight rollback or promise a refund.

## Workflow for future bookings

Use a durable orchestrator:

`START → RESERVE_REFUNDABLE_HOTEL → CONFIRM_FLIGHT → COMPLETED`

Validate that the hotel’s refund window covers flight confirmation and recovery. A definitive flight failure triggers `CANCEL_HOTEL → REFUND_PENDING → COMPENSATED_FAILED`; reconcile uncertain flight outcomes first. Flight confirmation is the irreversible pivot. If reversible holds are available, hold both resources before crossing that pivot.

Persist transitions and outgoing commands atomically through an outbox. Deduplicate replies and provider operations; carry `saga_id` throughout. Failed hotel cancellation/refund remains pending with retries, DLQ escalation, and an owner—never fabricated compensation success. Test duplicate delivery, lost replies, late hotel success, flight rejection, and cancellation failure. Monitor aged recovery states and refund deadlines.

## Guidance used and limits

Applied `SKILL.md` and `references/details.md`: centralized orchestration, durable step tracking, idempotency, per-step timeouts, correlation IDs, compensation testing, and escalation. Adapted the generic compensation rules because irreversible purchases cannot be guaranteed to compensate; transport failure also does not prove absence of side effects. This is a proposed workflow, not an executed integration. Airline terms, hotel refund timing, provider idempotency, and traveler authorization must be validated before implementation.
