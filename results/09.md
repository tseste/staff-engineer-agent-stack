Focus the next 90 days on an enterprise beachhead: teams sharing a repeated workflow, an identified buyer, and procurement compatible with runway. Defer consumer expansion and standalone AI. Enterprise’s 65% qualified-revenue share warrants investigation despite its 10% signup share; neither proves realized revenue or product-market fit. Self-serve’s 70% first-month churn makes acquisition expansion premature.

Assumptions: “qualified revenue” means pipeline; independent prospects share a problem; SSO blocks purchases; the core workflow delivers value; a narrow authentication integration fits six weeks. Staffing, deal sizes, enterprise retention, and sales cycles are unknown. All thresholds below are proposed.

Position around reliable completion of the chosen workflow with enterprise access controls. Validate differentiation against customers’ alternatives; SSO is a prerequisite.

Provisionally allocate engineering capacity: 60% enterprise enablement, 25% activation and retention, 15% reliability. Maintain one product; reject bespoke forks.

1. Days 1–14: instrument organization creation, first workflow completion, and subsequent completions with tenant, timestamp, and acquisition cohort. Define completion with users; exclude test activity. Build weekly organization retention and time-to-first-value reports. Sales verifies blockers, procurement dates, and paid-pilot terms with three independent prospects. Finance models cash receipts after sales delays against burn; pipeline cannot extend runway.
2. Days 15–45: after repeated SSO blockers and credible paid-pilot commitments, implement tenant-scoped SSO using buyers’ required protocol, verified domain ownership, account linking, admin recovery, and authentication audit events. Release behind tenant flags. Acceptance checks cover cross-tenant denial, invalid assertions, account takeover prevention, and recovery. Repair the largest observed activation failure in the shared workflow.
3. Days 46–90: onboard paid pilots. North Star: weekly customer organizations completing the core workflow. Guardrails: 30/60/90-day retention, collected cash, support cost, and customer concentration. Expand only if two independent pilots pay, sustain weekly use for four weeks, and support a viable cash forecast. Otherwise narrow scope and reassess before further spending. Revisit AI only against a validated workflow bottleneck.
