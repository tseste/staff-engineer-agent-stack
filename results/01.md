# Recommendation

Prioritize a bounded onboarding experiment; defer committing to an AI dashboard. Three AI mentions identify a discovery lead, not a validated requirement. Seven steps and 40 weekly setup tickets suggest friction, but do not establish why activation is 28%.

Assumptions: improving new-customer value is the objective; the two engineers have 40 combined working days; customer access is available. Activation’s definition, observation window, cohort size, ticket denominator, and prospect deal value remain unknown.

## Four-week work package

1. **Days 1–4: discovery and instrumentation, 8 engineer-days.** Engineer A verifies activation measurement and instruments onboarding step entry, completion, failure, and first-value completion. Engineer B groups setup tickets by failure and observes unsuccessful and successful setups. Interview support and affected users first, engineers/data owners next, then Sales and the product decision-maker. Interview all three prospects directly: reconstruct their last relevant task, workaround, business consequence, and what “AI dashboard” would enable. Seek three independent sources per proposed requirement; retain unsupported findings as hypotheses.
2. **Days 5–14: one intervention, 20 engineer-days.** Select the largest evidenced, addressable setup obstacle. Engineer A implements the backend change; Engineer B implements the flow and experiment assignment. Candidate changes include deferring unnecessary fields or making a failed setup step recoverable. Choose from observed failures. Deliver a feature-flagged slice, event schema, source-linked acceptance criteria, and rollback control. Candidate AC, **INTERPRETED—requires validation**: given a retryable setup failure, retry preserves accepted input and creates no duplicate resource. Test that failure path and first-value completion.
3. **Days 15–18: evaluate, 8 engineer-days.** Compare randomized eligible cohorts if traffic supports it. Proposed target: activation improves by 7 percentage points; setup tickets per 100 new accounts decline without increased setup errors. Preserve the activation window; report sample sizes and uncertainty. Inconclusive results require longer observation.
4. **Days 19–20: 4 engineer-days** for fixes, documentation, and the next decision.

## Evidence that changes the choice

Reprioritize if onboarding friction does not explain abandonment, or prospects demonstrate a shared costly task, commit to a pilot, and a task prototype outperforms a non-AI alternative. Validate data access, feasibility, and commercial benefit first. The product owner resolves revenue-versus-activation priorities.
