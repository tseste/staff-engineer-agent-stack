# Billing API program decision

**Assumptions:** Week 1 starts now; week 8 is the production deadline. A’s contract serves B–D. Assume C cannot deploy from week 6. Assignments and dates below are proposals.

**Decision:** Enter recovery; retain the deadline without claiming a credible commitment. Capacity and integration are red; schedule and outcome are at risk; D’s forecast and operational readiness are unknown. B’s acceptance is blocked by its failing integration test.

The outcome is four teams supporting the required API with correct production billing and operational handoff. Identify the accountable sponsor Monday; engineering leads retain technical decisions, release owners launch authority.

| Owner | This week’s action and required evidence |
|---|---|
| Sponsor + A’s engineering manager | Tuesday: allocate a named engineer and identify displaced work. Reprioritize for A; borrowing requires donor-team impact assessment. Without allocation, escalate deadline exposure immediately. |
| A lead + B–D leads | By Wednesday, record contract/version, compatibility rules, examples, consumer acceptance criteria, and unresolved decisions. Agree a dated handoff with consumer acknowledgment; contract readiness requires reviewed artifacts and executable contract tests. |
| B lead + test owner | By Thursday, reproduce and diagnose the failure, assign the fix, and provide passing integration evidence against the agreed contract. Keep acceptance blocked until verified. |
| C lead + release owner | Wednesday: confirm freeze scope and deployment lead time; reserve a week-5 release window and document rollback/support arrangements. |
| D lead | By Thursday, provide a bounded estimate, assumptions, staffing, dependencies, and earliest/latest delivery forecasts; timebox investigation of unknowns. |
| Program lead | Friday: publish one dependency record with provider, receiver, acceptance evidence, needed-by date, fallback, and escalation owner. Link actual artifacts when available. |

**Proposed sequencing:** contract → consumer validation → integrated billing checks → C release by week 5 → remaining releases by week 7 → week-8 contingency. Friday’s estimates must validate these targets. If infeasible, sponsor decides added capacity or an authorized compatibility bridge/freeze exception; none is assumed.

Billing operations owns reconciliation evidence, baselines, and support readiness. Review blockers daily this week.
