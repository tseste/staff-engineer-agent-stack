**Assumptions:** Usage means invocation counts, not unique users. Proposed retention/SLA values require adoption. Analytics is optional; geography/legal requirements are unspecified.

- **Purpose/classification:** Measure weekly usage by plan. Allowlist `{week_start_utc, feature_enum, plan_tier_enum}`; derive tier server-side. Reject unknown fields. Remove email, IP, URLs, reset tokens, and account IDs. Disable SDK enrichment and URL/referrer capture; prevent IP/header logging at collectors/proxies. Existing identifiers and temporary deletion handles are restricted personal data; tokens are secrets.
- **Lifecycle:** Backend increments per-user weekly counters under a random analytics-only deletion handle, mapped separately to identity. Use handles solely for deletion/cohort checks. No event archive. Expire contributions/mappings 14 days after week end; bound delivery retries to 24 hours. Keep finalized totals two years from week end only after assessing re-identification risk. Suppress cohorts below 20 contributors and prevent differencing; thresholding alone does not establish anonymity. Otherwise retain deletable contributions for only 14 days. Two-year raw-event retention lacks purpose.
- **Deletion:** Verified request, closure, or revocation blocks collection/replay. Resolve handles before deleting mappings; idempotently remove contributions and recompute identifiable totals across queues, caches, replicas, warehouse, exports, and processors within 72 hours. Track acknowledgments/retries. Only demonstrably anonymous totals remain. Isolate backups; expire within 30 days of deletion. Apply restricted tombstones before restored data becomes accessible; remove tombstones after copies/replay windows expire.
- **Access:** Pipeline writes; deletion service deletes; analysts read approved aggregates. Audit access monthly.
- **Tenant/isolation:** Server binds contribution access to tenant context; prohibit customer cross-tenant queries.
- **Residency:** Inventory transit, stores, processors, and backups; enforce a documented approved region set.
- **Consent:** No optional analytics before consent; revocation stops collection and triggers deletion.

**Acceptance/rollout:** Data engineering owns implementation. Seed synthetic secrets/users; require zero forbidden fields, unauthorized/cross-tenant reads, or pre-consent/post-revocation events. Verify region compliance, retention boundaries, zero subject records across stores within deadlines, correct recomputation, and no resurrection after backup restoration. Purge legacy telemetry; escalate exposed reset tokens for invalidation and incident assessment. Repeat deletion exercises quarterly.

**Guidance used and limitations:** Applied purpose-first minimization, classification, all seven privacy dimensions, telemetry consent and aggregation guidance, and the data-lifecycle, acceptance-criteria, and retention/deletion-verification templates. These are proposed requirements, not executed checks. Actual stores, processor capabilities, anonymity, residency constraints, and legal obligations remain unverified; legal interpretation belongs to counsel. Product must confirm invocation counts satisfy its measurement need.
