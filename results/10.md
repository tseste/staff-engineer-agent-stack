# Portfolio decision

Select maintenance (4 engineer-weeks), SSO (8), and search (6): **18 of 20**, leaving **2 for contingency**. Defer the 12-week redesign.

**Evidence:** Two signed contracts totaling $200k ARR depend on SSO; database support ends in two months. Redesign has no validation. The search benefit is an estimate, not measured savings.

**Assumptions:** Capacity excludes routine overhead; estimates include testing and rollout. SSO satisfies remaining contract conditions. Confirm staffing: engineer-weeks alone cannot establish deadline feasibility. Owners are proposed roles.

**Commitments recommended:** Allocate 12 weeks to maintenance and SSO; provisionally reserve 6 for search. Limit active bets to two, prioritizing maintenance during conflicts. Reversals risk unsupported operation or lost conditional revenue.

| Horizon / owner | Outcome and engineering scope | Confidence, dependencies, risk |
|---|---|---|
| Now: maintenance / platform lead | Move from impending support expiry to a supported database version; verify compatibility, rehearse restore/rollback, migrate, and validate workloads before support ends. | High necessity; medium delivery confidence. Depends on compatibility and migration testing; outage/data risk. |
| Now: SSO / identity lead | Move two contracts from SSO-blocked to customer acceptance, potentially unlocking $200k ARR; confirm identity-provider requirements, implement authentication, test authorization/session handling, and stage rollout. | High commercial evidence; medium delivery confidence. External acceptance dependency; security/integration risk. |
| Next: search / engineering lead | Measure baseline, target 20 fewer search-related support hours/week, instrument failures, validate causes, then implement improvements. Discovery fits within six weeks. | Low benefit confidence; depends on support evidence. Risk: savings fail to materialize. |

**Decision gates:** Continue maintenance when rehearsals pass; pause unsafe migration and revise, preserving support coverage. Continue SSO while acceptance requirements and contracts hold; pause unresolved dependencies; cancel if both contracts disappear without other justified demand. Revisit both weekly. Start search only after evidence supports its benefit; otherwise release its allocation.

**Options/scenarios:** If estimates overrun, use contingency, then defer search. If 20 weeks is gross capacity, 25% overhead leaves 15: fund maintenance plus SSO and retain 3. Keep redesign in Later until validated. Review Now monthly and Later quarterly.
