# Async export SLO

Assumptions: the ten-minute clock starts when the API accepts a valid export. Success means the complete, correct artifact is available through the customer’s download path. Use a rolling 28-day window; all accepted export types share this objective.

**SLI:** `good / eligible`. At time `t`, eligible exports have deadlines in `(t − 28 days, t]`, where `deadline = accepted_at + 10 minutes`. Good exports became successfully downloadable at or before their deadline. Pending, lost, failed, and late exports are bad. Exports younger than ten minutes remain pending evaluation. Deduplicate retries by logical export ID; worker retries never restart the clock. No cancellation exclusions are assumed.

Persist acceptance and artifact-ready timestamps durably. An independent deadline evaluator classifies every export exactly once, including jobs whose workers never report completion. Attribute results to the original deadline, backfill delayed evaluations, and reconcile counts against accepted jobs. Completion-only latency histograms would hide stuck work. POST’s 202 response and 40 ms latency measure acceptance, not this outcome.

**Objective:** at least **99% within ten minutes**. At 200 exports/day, 28 days contains approximately 5,600 exports and a budget of **56 misses**. Actual allowance is `floor(0.01 × eligible)`; remaining budget is `0.01 × eligible − bad`. Empty cohorts are “no data,” not 100% success.

**Initial alerts:**

- Page when any unfinished export is older than 15 minutes for five minutes, or at least three exports miss deadlines in one hour while overdue work remains. Include affected IDs, oldest age, queue depth, and worker health. This detects persistent stalls despite sparse traffic.
- Create a ticket when `burn = (bad / eligible) / 0.01` is at least 2 in both 24-hour and three-day windows, with at least three misses in 24 hours. Suppress ratio evaluation for empty windows. Avoid paging on a single short-window percentage spike.
- Alert on evaluator heartbeat missing for five minutes; show telemetry gaps as unknown.

Review isolated misses daily and budget weekly. At budget exhaustion, prioritize reliability and defer discretionary risky changes. Tune alert thresholds from observed arrival patterns and responder feedback.

**Skill guidance and limitations:** Applied user-perceived SLIs, expectation-based objectives, error budgets, multi-window burn alerts, and regular reviews from `SKILL.md` and `references/details.md`. Adapted windows and added direct overdue-work detection because traffic is sparse. These are proposed definitions and initial thresholds; no production measurements or tests were available. A separate acceptance-availability SLO would cover valid submissions rejected before acceptance.
